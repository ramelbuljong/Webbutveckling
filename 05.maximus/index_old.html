timeframe confirmation for entries. First, mark key levels on the 1H and 4H charts, such as previous session highs and lows, especially around New York close (4 pm) and late London hours (9-11 pm). These zones often act as liquidity pools. When price reaches a level, avoid entering immediately. Wait for a reaction, ideally a liquidity sweep where stops are taken before the real move begins. Drop to the 5-minute chart to look for structure shifts, such as a break of structure (BOS) or change of character (CHOCH), often accompanied by an inverse fair value gap (IFVG). Then refine on the 1-minute chart for confirmation: another BOS or CHOCH, a clear liquidity sweep with displacement, or price respecting the IFVG. Smart Money divergence (SMT) between correlated assets adds extra confluence. Stops are placed where the trade idea is invalid, usually beyond the liquidity sweep or IFVG zone. Take profit is set at the next 1H/4H level or previous session high/low. The key is combining time-based levels with structure breaks, liquidity concepts, and confirmation on smaller timeframes.
