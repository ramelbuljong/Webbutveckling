Crypto Explained

Cryptocurrency is a digital form of money used for online transactions. Unlike traditional money, crypto is decentralized and is not controlled by a bank or government.

Some of the most popular cryptocurrencies are Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana. Crypto markets are open 24/7 and are known for high volatility.
